# Constitution clause library (drafting starting points, not legal advice)

All of these are generated by scripts/build_pack.py from config flags. A lawyer must review them before adoption.

- Pre-emption on new issues (clause 4.1 to 4.4): offer to existing holders pro rata first. Waiver only if every entitled
  Member agrees in writing.
- Anti-dilution for founding shareholders (clause 4.5, flag `anti_dilution`): no new Shares, options or convertible securities
  without each Founding Member's written consent, unless each was first offered its full pro rata entitlement.
- Option over new Shares for a third party (clause 4.6, config `option`): carve-out from 4.1 to 4.5 for up to N Shares to the
  holder by an expiry date on the terms of a separate Option Deed. The price formula must be defined in the Option Deed.
  Sizing note: for the option holder to end up with one third, the option must be over half the existing share count
  (for example 60 new Shares on 120 existing gives 60 of 180).
- Shotgun buy-sell (clause 7.5, flag `shotgun`): two-party mechanism with a 30 day election, deemed sale on silence, 30 day
  completion, 12 month lockout. Needs rework once more than two holders exist.
- Shotgun with three or more holders: with three equal holders any two exceed an ordinary threshold above 50%, so ordinary
  decisions do not deadlock and the two-party clause is not needed for them. It still matters for decisions needing 75%
  (for example Special Resolutions), where two of three cannot pass the resolution. A common founder intent is that holders
  meeting the ordinary threshold may call a shotgun against the blocking minority. The generated clause 7.5 only covers two
  holders, so this extension must be drafted by a lawyer (who may call it, on whom, what triggers it, and how price is set).
- Voting (clause 14): one vote per Share on a show of hands and a poll. Ordinary resolution threshold from
  `vote_threshold_pct` of all votes (not only votes cast). Special Resolutions stay at the statutory 75% of votes cast.
- Deadlock note: with two equal holders and a threshold above 50%, both must agree. The shotgun clause is the exit.
- Directors: maximum from `max_directors`; Directors' quorum of two with a majority and no casting vote.
- Shareholders need not be directors (clause 5.1).

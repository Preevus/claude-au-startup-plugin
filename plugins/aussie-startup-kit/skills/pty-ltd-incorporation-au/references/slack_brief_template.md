# Slack brief template

Post to the channel the user names (find it with `slack_search_channels`; find people with `slack_search_users` and
mention them with <@USERID>). Plain "@claude" text may not trigger the Claude app, so tell the user that.

Sections: Done, Still needs actioning (with the owner and the blocker), Critical path, Needs a decision or check.
Include the Linear project link and the Drive folder link. Exclude Director IDs, dates of birth, addresses of individuals,
TFNs and bank details. Keep it short enough to read in a minute.

# Linear project blueprint: company incorporation (ACN and ABN)

Create one project named "<Company> Incorporation (ACN and ABN)" in the company's Linear team (check `list_teams`
and `list_projects` first so nothing is duplicated). Lead and assignee: the requesting user. Attach the Drive folder and
document links. Create 4 milestones, then the issues below with the milestone set, then add blocking links
(`blockedBy`) in a second pass once issue identifiers are known. Priorities: 1 = urgent, 2 = high, 3 = medium, 4 = low.

## Milestone 1: Pre-lodgement decisions and documents
- A. Decide company structure, officeholders and share structure (P2)
- B. Each director obtains a Director ID via ABRS (P1). Each person applies personally. Blocked by A.
- C. Check and confirm company name availability (P2)
- D. Confirm registered office and principal place of business, occupier's consent if needed (P2)
- E. Complete the company constitution: resolve all option clauses and schedules (P2). Blocked by A.
- F. Lawyer or compliance review of constitution and packs (P2). Blocked by E.
- G. Complete the ASIC registration information sheet (P2). Blocked by A, B, C, D.
- H. Prepare and sign consents to act and applications for shares (P2). Blocked by F, G.
- I. Choose lodgement route and confirm current ASIC fee (P3)
- J. (Optional) Draft shareholders' agreement or option deed if founders want shotgun, anti-dilution or an option holder (P2)

## Milestone 2: ASIC lodgement and ACN
- K. Lodge ASIC Form 201 and pay the registration fee (P1). Blocked by H, I.
- L. Receive ACN and certificate of registration, record corporate key securely (P1). Blocked by K.

## Milestone 3: ABN and tax registrations
- M. Apply for the company ABN via the Australian Business Register (P1). Blocked by L (unless the combined
  Business Registration Service route is confirmed to apply for the ABN in the same transaction).
- N. Confirm company TFN and verify the ABN on ABN Lookup (P2). Blocked by M.
- O. Decide and lodge GST and PAYG withholding registrations (P3). Blocked by M.
- P. Confirm public officer requirement and appoint if needed (P3). Blocked by L.

## Milestone 4: Post-registration corporate records
- Q. Sign the first directors' resolution (P2). Blocked by L, E.
- R. Issue share certificates and set up registers (P2). Blocked by Q.
- S. Decide on registered agent notification and non-trading declaration (P4)
- T. Open company bank account and deposit share capital (P3). Blocked by M.
- U. Diarise ASIC annual review and ongoing compliance (P4)

Each issue description should state the task, the document section it uses, and a "Done when" line. Never put Director IDs,
dates of birth or TFNs in Linear. When progress is made, set statuses (Done, In Progress, Backlog) and add a comment on
anything partly done saying exactly what is still missing.
